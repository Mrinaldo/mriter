# Privacy Policy for Mriter

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026  
**Application:** Mriter (The Novelist & Publishing Studio)  
**Developer:** Mrinaldo / Mriter Authors  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Mrinaldo/mriter

---

## 1. Overview
Mriter is designed as an offline-first, local-first manuscript writing and digital book publishing studio. Your privacy is paramount: your stories, manuscripts, characters, notes, and creative works belong entirely to you.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Data:** Mriter does not require an account, registration, login, email address, or name to use the software.
- **No Tracking or Telemetry:** Mriter contains no third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, advertising SDKs, or background telemetry.
- **No Cloud Uploads of Creative Work:** All manuscripts, story bibles, character outlines, and exports (EPUB, DOCX, JSON) are processed and saved solely to your local device file system. No manuscript text is sent to our servers or any cloud service unless you explicitly choose to store your local files in your own personal cloud drive (such as OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox).

## 3. Network and Internet Access
Mriter operates completely offline. Network connectivity is only utilized if you choose to download external web fonts or follow external hyperlinks provided in the documentation. No personal or manuscript data is transmitted across the internet.

## 4. Microsoft Store & Operating System Data
If downloaded via the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect diagnostic and telemetry data concerning app installation and crash diagnostics pursuant to the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/). Mriter developers do not receive individually identifiable information from these services.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the official GitHub repository:  
https://github.com/Mrinaldo/mriter/issues
