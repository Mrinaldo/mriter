Apple Design Ideology vs Traditional Writing Tools

Dimension Apple HIG Philosophy Linear / Notion Style Traditional Scrivener / Word
Chrome & Surface Vibrant frosted glass (`backdrop-blur-2xl`) with specular sheen Flat, opaque neutral panels with 1px border lines Dense, grey, multi-tiered ribbon toolbars
Mode Switching Tactile sliding segmented control with smooth spring physics Standard tab buttons with bottom active border Menu bar items or dense toolbar dropdowns
Document Architecture Native Proxy Icon (drag, rename, versions, iCloud status) Dropdown breadcrumb pill File menu bar items (`File > Save As...`)
Progress Tracking Circular Activity Rings (Apple Watch & Pages progress ring) Linear progress bar or raw numeric count Bottom status bar text label
Typography Dual pairing: SF Pro (Controls) + New York (Prose & Headings) Inter or Roboto throughout Times New Roman or Courier
